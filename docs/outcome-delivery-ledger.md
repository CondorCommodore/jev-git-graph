## Approval boundaries

The build is authorized. New Jev evidence requires an exact prepared request
manifest and approval. Real cleanup requires its concrete verified recovery
bundle and exact manifest approval. Neither boundary blocks independent build
work. A model answer cannot authorize deletion. Non-EXACT archive-backed deletion,
worktree removal and stash dropping require their own specified preservation
policy and explicit approval if they become necessary.
